## 1.4.0

- Added Yes Steve Model paper doll compatibility: DragonSurvival dragons
  now keep their neck/head in YSM's HUD paper doll and paper-doll config
  screen, and the YSM config preview uses the same compatibility path.
- Added YSM glide fixes: body orientation follows the paper doll view,
  the dragon no longer twists toward the take-off heading, and roll/bank
  is kept out of the preview to avoid the lock-south twist. Paper doll
  pitch wobble now works in YSM previews as well.
- Added a one-stop DragonSurvival / Yes Steve Model render priority
  switch (client config): two independent toggles choose the local
  player model and other players' models when both mods are installed.
  Defaults: self = DragonSurvival, others = YSM. The [ysm_compat]
  section is hidden when YSM is not installed.
- Added YSM UI preview scale isolation: YSM GUI and HUD previews use
  normal player scale/height; the in-world YSM model still follows the
  dragon's real scale.
- Added a client-side DragonSurvival bite-state reset for self=YSM,
  fixing the left/right click animation sticking on clients that render
  an YSM-model player as a DragonSurvival dragon.
- Fixed YSM model selection preview scaling inversely with dragon
  growth.
- Fixed DragonSurvival / Yes Steve Model render priority depending on
  mod load order; DragonSurvival now renders deterministically when
  selected.
